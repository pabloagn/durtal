**Type**: Feature
**Depends On**: None

## Overview
SLN-427. Pablo: "the publisher page is ugly and has no dynamism". A publisher page was a long text list: one bordered box per book with a small edition row, four tabs (All, Owned, Wanted, On order) and pagination. A house had no logo and no banner. The publisher cards on `/publishers` were text only.

Now a publisher page follows the author page: the house's banner behind the header, its logo shown whole beside the name, the facts and the actions, then the shared detail layout. Its books are a catalogue of book cards with this house's edition covers, in grid or list view, with search, filters, sort and pagination on the server and in the URL. The record column holds the counts and the details. The cards on `/publishers` lead with the logo.

## Implementation Details
- Migration `0053_publisher_backgrounds` (generated by drizzle-kit on `main`): `media_type_check` no longer bans backgrounds for organizations; art objects and perfume variants keep the ban. `supportsMediaType()` follows. Other open PRs also carry a `0053` (#10, #12, SLN-414's branch): whichever merges later rebases and regenerates its migration.
- `src/lib/publishers/books.ts` (new): `parsePublisherBookQuery()` reads the URL (`q`, `state`, `mark`, `language`, `binding`, `yearMin`, `yearMax`, `author`, `imprint`, `sort`, `order`, `page`, `perPage`; the old `?filter=` tab links still work). `getPublisherBooks()` picks one edition per book from the house and its imprints (owned first, then one with a cover, then the earliest), filters, sorts (title, author, year, recent) and pages in SQL. `getPublisherBookFacets()` gives each filter's values with book counts; `getPublisherCounts()`, `getPublisherTargets()` and `getPublisherImages()` feed the record column, the wanted list and the header.
- `src/app/publishers/[slug]/page.tsx`: banner (`FullBleedLayer`, as on the author page), `PublisherHeader`, About in `<Prose>`, notes, the books, books wanted from the house; record column with the counts, details and website. New `loading.tsx` and `not-found.tsx`; an empty state for a house with no books and `NoResults` for a filter that matches nothing.
- `src/components/publishers/publisher-header.tsx` (new): the logo on a dark tile (`object-contain`, never cropped; the shared `Monogram` when there is none), the name with the favourite star, Edit and the actions menu on its cap-height center (`CapAlignedControls`), Manage media.
- `src/components/publishers/publisher-books.tsx` (new): the filter row (`EntityFilters` with the shared `FilterDropdown`, a year range) and the grid or list (`LibraryView`), with their own view and size preferences.
- `src/components/books/media-manager-dialog.tsx`, `src/components/media/upload-zone.tsx`: owner `organization`, with a Logo tab (square, shown whole) and a Background tab; the drop prompt names the image ("Drop logo image here").
- `/publishers`: `getPublishers()` returns each house's active logo; `PublisherCard` and the list row lead with it.
- Docs: `docs/02_DATA_MODEL.md` (media types), `docs/03_DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md` (publisher page), `docs/04_ROUTES_AND_VIEWS.md` (Publishers, Publisher Detail).
- `getPublisherCatalogue()` stays: the page no longer uses it, and its integration tests still cover the house-family and wanted rules the new query copies.
