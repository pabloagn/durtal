# Task 0269: Mixed Collections Without Losing Edition Choices (SLN-362)

**Status**: In Progress
**Created**: 2026-10-04
**Priority**: MEDIUM
**Type**: Feature
**Depends On**: SLN-347, SLN-354
**Blocks**: SLN-375, SLN-379, SLN-380

## Overview
A collection can now hold whole works of every open collection beside the
book editions it already held: a film, a perfume, a painting, or a book with
no edition chosen. Members of every kind share one order, can be added,
removed and reordered, and show as cards of their own kind. Existing edition
members and their order are unchanged, and no placeholder edition is made.

## Implementation Details

**Data** (docs 02, `collection_works`): `collection_works (collection_id,
work_id, sort_order, added_at)`, PK on the pair, both keys CASCADE. Migration
`0057_collection_works`, generated by drizzle-kit on top of
`0056_publisher_backgrounds`. `sort_order` runs across `collection_editions`
and `collection_works`, so the collection keeps one order.

**The duplicate rule**: a book in a collection both as a whole book and
through one or more editions shows as its editions (the edition choice stays,
and the card says "also collected as the book") and counts once. Removing the
last such edition shows the whole book again. `shownMembers`
(`src/lib/collections/members.ts`) and `collectionCounts`
(`src/lib/collections/counts.ts`) hold this rule for the page, counts, moves
and previews.

**Services** (`src/lib/actions/collections.ts`): `bulkAddWorksToCollection`
(open kinds only), `removeWorksFromCollection` and `moveCollectionMember`
(either kind; `moveCollectionEdition` now moves within the one order), with
activity events like editions. `createCollection` takes whole works too.
`getCollections` and `getCollectionsForWork` (now with `holdsWork`) carry the
counts; `getCollectionCoverPreviews` takes whole works' images in the one
order; `getCollectionSelection` returns `works` (other kinds and books with no
edition) instead of `withoutEditions`; `deleteCollection` records the removal
for whole works too; `searchWorksForCollection` finds works of one open kind.

**Screens**:
- `/collections/[id]`: one ordered list of `MemberCard`s (edition, whole book,
  film, perfume, painting), each with its own image shape, fixed text lines,
  move and remove; the header counts books, editions, films, perfumes and
  paintings.
- "Add" (was "Add books"): Editions, Books, Films, Perfumes, Paintings.
- Collection cards and the library selection dialog count members once
  ("12 editions", or "5 items" when whole works are in); the selection dialog
  adds a book with no edition as a whole book instead of refusing it.
- Film, perfume and painting pages: "Collections" in the actions menu.

**Elsewhere**: similar books ("More from these collections") count a whole
book like its editions; the harmonization "empty collection" check counts
whole works; work merges carry `collection_works` as a membership link.
